# SQL Structuring and Visualizing Data for Analytics

Welcome to the **SQL Analytics Portfolio**! This repository contains a series of end-to-end SQL case studies and business analytics investigations using the **North American Stock Market 1990–2023** dataset. The projects here explore corporate performance, CEO compensation, R&D investment, and firm-specific behavior over time.

Each folder in this repo is a standalone case study, complete with SQL code, data dictionaries, and README documentation.

## Project Catalog

### Corporate Financial Analysis

- **Amazon_ROA_Analysis Project**  
  Investigates Amazon’s ROA and compares it to industry benchmarks.  
  `roa_analysis_amzn_us.sql`

- **ROA US Firms Employee Analysis Project**  
  Connects average ROA with employment metrics across firms.  
  `roa_emp_study_us_firms.sql`

- **Top 500 Sales ROA Analysis Project**  
  Tracks long-term performance of the 500 largest firms by 1990 sales.  
  `top500_us_firm_roa_tracking.sql`

- **US Firm Analysis Project**  
  Broad queries examining firm longevity, size, and sales efficiency.  
  `us_firm_analysis.sql`

---

### CEO & Executive Compensation

- **CEO Pay and Total Q Analysis Project**  
  Explores how CEO pay correlates with total q and profitability.  
  `ceo_pay_and_qtot_study.sql`

- **Executive Compensation and CEO Analysis Project**  
  Breaks down executive roles, age, pay, and NAICS classification.  
  `execucomp_ceo_analysis.sql`

- **Firms Specific Profit and Executive Compensation Analysis Project**  
  Links long-term CEO consistency to firm-specific profitability.  
  `fsp_and_exec_consistency.sql`

---

### Firm Behavior & Profitability

- **Firm-Specific Profit Volatility US Firms Project**  
  Examines profit consistency and volatility across years.  
  `fsp_volatility_us_firms.sql`

- **Firm Specific Profits Outperformance Analysis Project**  
  Identifies firms that outperformed their peers over a decade.  
  `fsp_outperformance_study.sql`

- **Total Decile to ROA Predictive Power Analysis Project**  
  Uses Total q deciles from 2014 to predict ROA in 2023.  
  `qtot_predictive_power_study.sql`

---

### Industry Structure & Dynamics

- **Firm Industry Analysis Project**  
  Tracks how many firms change NAICS classifications.  
  `industry_shift_us_firms_case.sql`

- **Firm Lifespan and Sales Power Project**  
  Examines firm survival and size longevity across decades.  
  `firm_lifespan_and_sales_power.sql`

- **Sales Concentration and ROA Gap Project**  
  Measures sales concentration over time and ROA inequality.  
  `sales_concentration_and_roa_gap.sql`

---

### Innovation & Intangibles

- **R&D Stock US Analysis Project**  
  Calculates a time-depreciated stock of R&D investment for 2023.  
  `rd_stock_analysis_us_2023.sql`
